# V2 and V3 Features

## V2

- household collaboration and multi-user meal planning
- shared recipe library
- favorites and recent recipes
- pantry tracking and ingredient inventory
- dietary filters and allergy-aware search
- leftover-aware meal planning
- grocery list by aisle or store section
- recipe sharing between household members

## V3

- AI-assisted meal planning recommendations
- automatic grocery budget tracking
- pantry-to-grocery optimization
- recurring weekly meal plans
- recipe suggestions based on inventory and preference history
- deeper nutrition and health tracking
- household shopping coordination across multiple users
