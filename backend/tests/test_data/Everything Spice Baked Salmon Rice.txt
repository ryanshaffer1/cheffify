"Everything Spice" Baked Salmon Rice Bowl with Avocado & Tomatoes
30 minutes | 2 servings
Ingredients
½ avocado
1 tbsp capers
½ small bunch fresh dill
1 pint grape tomatoes
½ cup jasmine rice
1 lemon
¾ lb salmon fillet
black pepper
Dijon mustard
extra virgin olive oil
garlic powder
onion powder
poppy seeds
salt
sesame seeds
Cookware
baking sheet pan
chef's knife
citrus juicer (optional)
colander
cutting board
grater (optional)
measuring cups
measuring spoons
mixing bowls
oven mitts
small saucepan
stirring spoon
Instructions
1 Preheat oven to 450°F.
2 Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add
water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
½ cup jasmine rice
1 cup water
3 Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.
1 pint grape tomatoes
1 lemon
½ small bunch fresh dill
½ avocado
4 Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is
fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
5 While the rice is cooking, transfer tomatoes to a baking sheet pan. Drizzle with oil and season with salt. Stir to coat and
spread out in an even layer.
2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
¼ tsp salt
6 Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and roast, stirring halfway through, until tomatoes are
just beginning to burst, about 10 minutes.
7 Meanwhile, place seeds and spices in a small bowl; stir to combine the "everything spice" mixture.
2 tsp sesame seeds
1 tsp poppy seeds
¼ tsp garlic powder
¼ tsp onion powder
¼ tsp black pepper
8 Pat the salmon dry with paper towels, season with salt, and place, skin-side down, on a plate. Spread the top-side with
Dijon, then sprinkle evenly with the spice mixture, pressing gently to adhere. (Reserve bowl for later use.)
¾ lb salmon fillet
¼ tsp salt
1 tbsp Dijon mustard
9 When the tomatoes are starting to burst, remove from the oven. Add salmon to the baking sheet, nestling the fillets into
the tomatoes.
10 Return baking sheet to the oven and continue to roast until the tomatoes are browned and salmon is cooked through,
7-8 minutes more. Remove from oven.
11 Meanwhile, zest and juice lemon into the reserved bowl.
12 Shave dill leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl with the lemon along with
oil, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine the dressing and set aside.
1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
⅛ tsp salt
⅛ tsp black pepper
13 Halve and pit avocado. Halve lengthwise and slice thinly while still in the skin, then scoop out with a spoon.
14 To serve, divide rice between bowls. Top with salmon, roasted tomatoes, avocado, and capers, then drizzle with
dressing. Enjoy!
1 tbsp capers
